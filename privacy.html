Privacy Policy

PPH PM Portal · Patrick's Plumbing & Heating, Inc. · Last updated October 12, 2026

PPH PM Portal is the private scheduling and maintenance app used by Patrick's Plumbing & Heating, Inc. ("we") to run our own preventive-maintenance work. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

What we collect

Customer names, service addresses, phone numbers and email addresses; details about the equipment we service (type, make, model, serial number, filter size, photos); visit records, checklists and notes; and signatures customers give to approve work. From QuickBooks Online we read and create customers, invoices and payment status for our own company file only.

How we use it

To schedule visits, send reminders and visit reports, keep each unit's service history, and create and collect invoices. We do not sell, rent or share customer information with anyone for marketing.

Where it is kept

Records are kept in our company's Google account and our QuickBooks Online company. Connection keys are stored privately in our script settings and are never shown in the app. Card payments are handled entirely by QuickBooks Payments; we never see or store card numbers.

Sticker pages

Anyone who scans the QR sticker on a unit sees that unit's filter size, service dates and work done, with technicians' first names. It never shows the homeowner's name, address or contact details.

Your choices

Customers can ask us to stop reminders, correct their information, or delete their records by calling or emailing us.
